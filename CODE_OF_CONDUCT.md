# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

We want Musiclify to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

**Do:** be respectful and patient, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, focus on what is best for the project and its users, and take responsibility for your mistakes.

**Don't:** harass, insult, or demean anyone; make sexualised comments or advances; publish anyone's private information; or behave in any way that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers by opening a [private security advisory](https://github.com/reshuk-code/musiclify/security/advisories/new) and noting that it is a conduct report. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
